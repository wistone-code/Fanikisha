This policy sets the rules for what organisers may do on Fanikisha and, above all, whom they may message and how. It protects guests from unwanted messages and protects every organiser's ability to reach their guests. Breaking it can lead to messages being held and accounts being suspended.

## 1. Scope

This policy applies to every organiser, team member and door-staff user, and to guests who upload content. It forms part of the [Terms of Service](/terms). "Message" means any SMS, email, WhatsApp message or card link sent through or prepared by Fanikisha.

## 2. The five rules for every message

1. **Only people you know and have a reason to invite.** Message only people you invited to your event or who you have a genuine existing relationship with for it, such as guests, contributors, committee members and service providers.
2. **Only about your event.** Invitations, RSVP and seating information, event-day notices, pledge and payment reminders, and thank-you notes. No adverts, no promotions of other products, no chain messages.
3. **Say who you are.** The message must make clear which event and which organiser it comes from. Never impersonate another person, family, church, company or bank.
4. **Be honest.** Dates, venue, amounts and links must be accurate. Never mislead people about what a contribution is for.
5. **Stop when asked.** If someone asks not to be messaged, stop at once and do not add them back.

## 3. Consent and lawful basis

3.1 The Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 requires consent that is specific, informed and freely given where consent is the basis for processing. Pre-ticked boxes, silence and unrelated past contact do not count.

3.2 Before you add a guest's number you must hold their consent or another lawful basis to contact them about this event. A guest who gave you their number so you could invite them to your event, or who confirms by reply, has consented for that event.

3.3 Do not buy, rent or scrape phone numbers. Do not upload a list you copied from a group, a directory or another organiser's event.

3.4 Keep a record of how you got each number (for example "gave number at the family meeting on 4 October" or a screenshot of their reply). We may ask you for it if someone complains. WhatsApp's business policy requires the same: a clear statement that the person is opting in to messages, the name of the sender, and compliance with applicable law.

3.5 You may use this wording when collecting numbers.

> **English:** "I agree that [ORGANISER NAME] may send me SMS or WhatsApp messages about [EVENT NAME] (invitation, reminders and thank-you) using Fanikisha. I can ask to stop at any time."
>
> **Kiswahili:** "Nakubali [JINA LA MWANDAAJI] anitumie ujumbe wa SMS au WhatsApp kuhusu [JINA LA TUKIO] (mwaliko, vikumbusho na shukrani) kupitia Fanikisha. Naweza kuomba kuacha wakati wowote."

## 4. What you may send

| Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- |
| Invitation and card link | Advertising or promotion of anything not part of your event |
| RSVP reminders to guests who have not replied | Messages to people who asked to stop |
| Seating, venue, directions and event-day notices | Messages to numbers you do not have a reason to contact |
| Pledge and payment reminders to people who pledged | Threats, shaming, or pressure to pay; messages about debts to people who did not pledge |
| Thank-you messages after the event | Chain messages, lotteries, get-rich offers or requests to forward |
| Replies to guests' own questions | Requests for PINs, passwords, card numbers or one-time codes |

## 5. Content rules

Do not send or upload content that is unlawful, threatening, harassing, defamatory, hateful, sexually explicit, or that exploits or endangers a child; that infringes copyright or privacy; that contains malware or links to scams or phishing pages; or that falsely appears to come from a bank, mobile-money provider, government body or another organisation. Never ask anyone for their mobile-money PIN or a one-time code. Do not use a link shortener that hides where a link leads.

## 6. Sending practices

- **Frequency.** Do not send more than three messages to a guest about one event without a reason, and avoid repeated reminders to people who have already replied. The automatic reminders send each guest at most once.
- **Timing.** Send between 07:00 and 21:00 East Africa Time unless the matter is urgent for the event.
- **Sender name.** Use a clear sender name. Do not register or use a sender ID that imitates another organisation.
- **Language.** Write so the guest can understand, in Kiswahili or English, as the guest prefers.
- **Quality.** If many recipients block, report or complain about your messages, we may hold your sending while we look into it.

## 7. Opting out

7.1 Any recipient can stop messages by using the "Stop messages" button on their card, by using our [data request page](/data-request), by telling the organiser, or by writing to %EMAIL% with their number.

7.2 When you learn of a request, stop messaging that person the same day. When we learn of it, we add the number to our suppression list. We check the list before every message we send and will not send to a suppressed number even if an organiser asks.

7.3 An opt-out stays in force until the person clearly opts back in. You must not tell guests they cannot opt out.

## 8. WhatsApp

8.1 Today the WhatsApp button prepares a message that you send yourself from your own WhatsApp account. You must follow WhatsApp's own terms and spam rules, including not sending bulk or automated messages from a personal account.

8.2 If we introduce messaging through the WhatsApp Business Platform, additional rules apply: you may send only to people who have opted in, using approved message templates; each template must match its approved purpose; replies from guests open a limited window in which you may respond freely; and Meta may rate-limit or block accounts with poor quality.

## 9. Photo wall and uploads

Upload only photos and videos you took or have the right to share, taken at the event, and not showing someone in a way they would object to. Do not upload images of children without a parent's or guardian's agreement. Any guest can report a photo; a photo reported three times is hidden until the organiser reviews it. The organiser may delete any photo and may turn the wall off at any time.

## 10. Security and misuse

Do not share your sign-in, test or scan other people's card links, attempt to guess codes, overload the Service, probe for weaknesses or access data that is not yours. Report any weakness you find to %EMAIL%; we will not take action against a good-faith report.

## 11. Reporting and enforcement

11.1 Anyone can report a message or content to %EMAIL%. We review reports promptly.

11.2 Depending on the seriousness and history, we may remove content, hold or limit your sending, ask you to show your consent records, suspend or close your account, and report conduct to the authorities. Where harm is serious or ongoing, we may act without notice.

11.3 We cooperate with the Personal Data Protection Commission, the Tanzania Communications Regulatory Authority, mobile operators and Meta where they investigate misuse.

## 12. Changes

We may update this policy; the current version is always on our website with its date. Material changes will be notified to organisers.
