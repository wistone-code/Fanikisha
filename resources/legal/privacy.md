This policy explains what personal data Fanikisha collects from event organisers and their guests, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and how to ask us to change or delete it. Fanikisha only messages people an organiser has added, only about that organiser's event, and never sells personal data.

## 1. Who we are and our roles

Fanikisha is a web application operated by **%LEGAL_NAME%**%REG_LINE%, of %ADDRESS% ("Fanikisha", "we", "us"). Our contact for privacy matters is our Data Protection Officer at %EMAIL%.

Two roles matter under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 ("PDPA"):

- **Organiser accounts.** For the account holders themselves (organisers, their team members and door staff) we are the **data controller**.
- **Guest and contribution data.** The organiser decides which guests to invite, which pledges to record and what to send them. For that data the organiser is the controller and Fanikisha is the **data processor**, acting on the organiser's instructions under our Organiser Data Processing Agreement. We also keep limited technical data about card openings and security for our own purposes, as controller.

## 2. Personal data we collect

| Who | Data | Where it comes from |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organisers, team members, door staff | Name, username, phone number, email address (optional), role, password (stored only as a one-way hash), sign-in times | The person, or the organiser or administrator who created the account |
| Guests and invitees | Name, phone number, party size, table or seat, meal choice, RSVP answer and notes, whether and when the card link was opened, check-in time and door | The organiser; the guest when they respond or arrive |
| Contributors | Name, phone number, pledged and paid amounts, payment dates and notes | The organiser |

| Photo wall contributors | Photos or videos they upload, optional name | The guest, through the event's wall link |
| Anyone opening a card or page | A device cookie and a hashed device identifier, browser and device type, time of visit; our hosting provider's server logs record the IP address | Automatically |

We do not collect payment-card numbers, mobile-money PINs, national ID numbers or biometric data. We do not ask for sensitive personal data. If an organiser enters it, they are responsible for having a lawful basis to do so, and we ask them not to.

## 3. Why we use personal data and our legal basis

| Purpose | Basis under the PDPA |
| --- | --- |
| Create and run organiser accounts; authenticate sign-in; keep accounts secure | Performance of our contract with the organiser; our legitimate interest in security |
| Create invitation cards, record RSVPs, run seating and check-in, track pledges, produce reports | The organiser's instructions; the guest's consent or the organiser's legitimate interest in running the event |
| Send invitations, reminders and thank-you messages at the organiser's request | The recipient's consent, which the organiser confirms they hold before adding the number (see our Acceptable Use and Messaging Policy) |
| Count card openings once per device and detect abuse or sharing of cards | Our legitimate interest in accurate reporting and fraud prevention |
| Show and moderate the photo wall | The uploader's consent, given at upload |
| Meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests, establish or defend legal claims | Legal obligation; legitimate interest |

We do not use personal data for advertising, we do not build profiles for sale and we do not make decisions about people by automated means that significantly affect them.

## 4. Messages (SMS, email and WhatsApp) and how to opt out

- We send a message only when an organiser asks us to, to a number or address the organiser added, and only about that organiser's event or pledge: invitations, reminders, event-day notices and thank-you notes. These are not marketing messages, and we do not use guest numbers for any other purpose.
- **SMS** is sent through our SMS provider. **Email** (for example password-reset codes) is sent through our email provider. **WhatsApp:** today an organiser can open a pre-written WhatsApp message from their own phone, and the message is then sent by them under WhatsApp's terms. If we later use the WhatsApp Business Platform, messages will be sent from our verified business account only to people who have opted in, and each will say who is sending it.
- **To stop messages:** use the "Stop messages" button on your invitation card, use our [data request page](/data-request), tell the organiser, or write to %EMAIL% with your phone number. We add the number to a suppression list that we check before every message we send, and we act on written requests within 2 working days.
- System emails, such as password resets, carry a notice that they come from a no-reply address.

## 5. Photos, media and other uploads

Guests who open an event's photo-wall link may upload photos or short videos. The organiser chooses whether the wall is open, PIN-protected or limited to invited guests. Anyone can report a photo; a photo reported three times is hidden automatically until the organiser reviews it. Uploaders must have the right to share the file and must not upload content that shows or targets a person without their consent. Organisers may also upload music or a custom card design. Uploaded files are stored in our database and are reachable only through the event's links and the organiser's account. The organiser can delete any photo at any time.

## 6. Cookies and similar technologies

We use only the cookies and browser storage needed to run the service. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

| Name or type | Purpose | Lifetime |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Session cookie and security (CSRF) token | Keeps an organiser signed in and protects forms from forgery | Until sign-out or the inactivity limit |
| Remember-me cookie | Keeps an organiser signed in on a device only if they tick "Remember me" | Until sign-out or expiry |
| Device cookie (fk_d) | Recognises a device that opened a card so that openings are counted once per device | Up to one year; the related records are deleted after 30 days |
| Browser storage and offline cache | Lets the door check-in tool keep working without signal and speeds up pages; may hold the event's guest list on door staff's phone until "Clear offline data" is used | Until cleared |

Pages load fonts and icons from Google Fonts and the Cloudflare content network, and the card design page may load map tiles and address search from OpenStreetMap. Those providers receive your IP address and browser details when a page loads. You can block cookies in your browser settings, but sign-in and check-in will then not work.

## 8. Transfers outside Tanzania

Our hosting and some of our providers are outside Tanzania, so personal data is transferred abroad. We make these transfers only as the PDPA allows, where the destination protects data adequately or another lawful ground applies, such as consent or the need to perform our contract with the organiser, and with any permit the Personal Data Protection Commission requires. We require each provider to protect the data to a standard no lower than the PDPA.

## 9. How long we keep personal data

| Data | How long we keep it |
| --- | --- |
| Organiser account details | While the account is active, then deleted within 90 days of closure |
| Event data (guests, RSVPs, pledges, seating, committee and provider details) | Until the organiser deletes it or the account is closed, then deleted within 90 days |
| Photos, music and custom designs | Until the organiser or uploader deletes them, or the event data is deleted |
| Card-view records (hashed device identifier and time) | 30 days, then deleted automatically |
| Card-open counters and failed-scan counters kept on a guest record | With the guest record |
| Suppression list (numbers that asked to stop) | For as long as needed so that we honour the request |
| Server and security logs | Up to 90 days |
| Backups | Overwritten on the provider's backup cycle shortly after deletion |
| Records we must keep by law | For the period the law requires |

Organisers are responsible for deleting event data they no longer need; we recommend within six months after the event.

## 10. Security

We protect personal data with technical and organisational measures that fit the risk: HTTPS for all connections; passwords stored only as one-way hashes; role-based access (administrator, team member, door staff); automatic sign-out after inactivity; limits on repeated sign-in and code attempts; revocable, non-guessable card links; and restricted access to our own systems. If we become aware of a breach that puts personal data at risk, we will contain it, notify the Personal Data Protection Commission promptly as the PDPA requires, and tell affected organisers without undue delay so they can inform their guests. No online service can be perfectly secure.

## 11. Your rights and how to use them

You may ask us to tell you what personal data we hold about you; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to its use; give you a copy; stop messaging you; or withdraw consent you gave. Use our [data request page](/data-request) or write to %EMAIL%, and tell us the phone number or name involved so we can find it. We may ask you to confirm who you are. We aim to answer within 30 days. Where we hold the data on an organiser's behalf we may pass your request to the organiser, and we will tell you if we do. We will refuse or limit a request only where the law allows, for example where we must keep a record by law, and we will explain why.

## 12. Children

Fanikisha is for adults organising events. We do not knowingly collect data directly from children. Organisers may list a child as part of a guest's party; they must have the parent's or guardian's authority to do so and should enter only a name or a headcount.
